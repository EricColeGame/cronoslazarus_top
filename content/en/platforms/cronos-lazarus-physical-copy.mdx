## What Cronos: Lazarus Brings to the Table

Before you go chasing discs, it helps to know exactly what you'd be putting on your shelf. Cronos: Lazarus continues the story of the wastelands of New Dawn, but it flips the perspective. You play as the once-loyal Pathfinder who is slowly becoming the Warden — isolated in the Terminal, cut off from the Collective, and obsessively trying to revive a lost Essence he refuses to let go.

The gameplay shifts with the character. Where the base game rewarded patience, positioning, and picking your fights carefully, the expansion leans into aggression. You get tools reserved for a Pathfinder: decoys you can leave behind to misdirect pursuers, and the ability to go temporarily invisible. Those abilities exist for a reason — a hunter engineered specifically to eliminate you is on your trail, and the encounters scale up into large, cinematic fights.

- **Tone shift:** faster, more aggressive combat than the base game
- **New abilities:** decoys, temporary invisibility, enhanced traversal
- **New antagonist:** a relentless, adaptive pursuer built to match the Warden
- **Story thread:** fragments of the Warden's past and the Lazarus Protocol itself

### Red flags worth memorizing

Community reports from disc collectors follow a predictable pattern: the listings that burn people are rarely outright scams, they're just vague. Use this checklist before you commit.

| Check | Good sign | Red flag |
|---|---|---|
| Product photo | Actual disc, case, and rating logo | Stock render with no case shown |
| Description wording | "Includes disc" or "disc version" | "Digital delivery," "code only," "no disc" |
| Price | In line with comparable retail discs | Dramatically below every other listing |
| Seller history | Long track record with reviews | Brand-new account, no feedback |
| Region info | Clear ESRB, PEGI, or CERO marking | Vague "import" language with no region stated |
| Platform labeling | Explicit platform name on the box | Generic "console" wording |

If a seller can't answer "is there a disc in the box?" in one sentence, walk away. That single question filters out most fake Cronos Lazarus physical copy listings.

## Frequently Asked Questions About the Cronos Lazarus Physical Copy

### Is there a Cronos Lazarus physical copy for PS5?

Not at the time of writing. The expansion is distributed digitally, and no standalone PlayStation 5 disc has been announced. If a Cronos Lazarus physical copy does appear for PS5, it will most likely arrive as part of a bundled complete edition rather than as a solo disc.

### Will a Cronos Lazarus physical copy include the base game?

Unknown, because no disc version has been announced. Based on how expansions usually ship, a standalone disc would probably still require Cronos: The New Dawn to play, while a complete edition disc would bundle both. Always read the box copy before assuming.

### Can I resell or lend a Cronos Lazarus physical copy?

If a disc version exists, resale and lending generally work the way they do for any console game. Digital purchases can't be resold or lent, which is one of the strongest reasons collectors keep waiting for a Cronos Lazarus physical copy instead of buying a download.

### How do I avoid fake "physical copy" listings?

Ask three questions: does the photo show a disc, does the description say "disc included," and does the box carry a visible age rating? If any answer is no, treat it as a code-in-a-box product. Player experience in collector communities is consistent on this — the cheapest listing is almost always the one that isn't a disc.
