## The Warden Unleashed: Combat, Story, and a New Predator

This is where **Cronos: Lazarus** earns the "brutal" label in its own description. The expansion puts you in the boots of the Warden — the character you met in the base game — during the period when he was still a loyal Pathfinder, mid-transformation.

The setup: isolated in the Terminal and cut off from the Collective, the Warden pours everything into reviving a lost Essence he refuses to let go. Then his sanctuary is compromised, and a new kind of hunter is deployed with a single instruction — find him and eliminate him.

Mechanically, the tone shifts hard:

| Element | Cronos: The New Dawn | Cronos: Lazarus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Combat philosophy | Precision, picking your battles | Faster, more aggressive pressure |
| Your role | Pathfinder | One of the Collective's elite agents |
| Signature abilities | Base-game toolkit | Decoys, temporary invisibility, enhanced traversal |
| Primary threat | Deliberate, tactical encounters | A relentless, adaptive pursuer built to match you |
| Encounter scale | Measured | Large-scale, cinematic set pieces |

That last row matters most. The expansion introduces an adversary engineered specifically to counter the Warden — highly adaptive, capable of matching his strength, and designed to escalate fights into set pieces rather than skirmishes. Pair that with tools like leaving a decoy in your wake or blinking out of enemy sight for a few seconds, and you get a version of Cronos that rewards mobility and misdirection over patient cover-shooting.

Story-wise, the expansion is framed as the Lazarus Protocol: fragments of the Warden's past, a revival mission he'll pursue at any cost, and a decision about how far he's willing to go to defy the Collective. If you cared about the lore of New Dawn, this is the chapter that fills in the gap between the Pathfinder you played and the Warden you met.

## FAQ

**Is the Cronos Lazarus wishlist the same as a preorder?**

No. Wishlisting is free and non-binding — it just adds the expansion to your Steam wishlist and notifies you when it becomes purchasable. A preorder is an actual transaction. At the time of writing, the DLC was listed as not yet available on Steam, so wishlisting was the only option.

**Do I need Cronos: The New Dawn to play Cronos: Lazarus?**

Yes. Steam lists the expansion as content that requires the base game in order to play. If you don't own The New Dawn yet, wishlist both — and check whether a bundle is available before buying them separately.

**What does a Cronos Lazarus wishlist notification actually look like?**

You'll typically receive an email from Steam, a notification in the desktop client, and an alert in the Steam mobile app, depending on your settings. The notification links directly to the store page, so you skip the search step entirely.

**When does Cronos: Lazarus release, and is the date final?**

The store page lists December 3, 2026 as the planned release date. Release dates can shift, which is another argument for a **Cronos Lazarus wishlist** entry — if the date moves, the notification moves with it.

**Will the expansion run on my PC?**

Check the minimum specs table above. The headline requirements are a GTX 1080-class GPU, 16 GB of RAM, and 30 GB of free storage on top of the base game. If you're below that, plan an upgrade before December rather than after.
