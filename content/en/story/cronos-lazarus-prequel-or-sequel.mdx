## The Case for a Prequel — and the Case for a Sequel

The prequel camp points out that expansions are a natural home for background material: origins, earlier expeditions, the events that set the main story in motion. Prequel-style DLC lets a studio explore lore without committing to a full release, and it's cheaper to produce because the engine, assets, and systems already exist.

The sequel camp counters that a Fall 2026 window lands well after the base game's launch, which is exactly when studios typically extend a story forward rather than backward. A forward-moving expansion also has a commercial advantage: it gives returning players a reason to reinstall and keeps momentum alive for a possible full sequel later.
