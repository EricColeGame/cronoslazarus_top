## Combat, Abilities, and the New Pursuer

This is where the expansion diverges most sharply from the base game. Cronos: The New Dawn rewarded patience — lining up shots, choosing engagements, retreating when the math didn't work. Lazarus flips that. You're playing as one of the Collective's elite agents, and the toolkit reflects it.

The headline abilities are a decoy you can leave in your wake and a temporary invisibility effect that lets you slip out of an enemy's line of sight. Both are designed to reward repositioning rather than turtling, and both pair naturally with the expansion's enhanced traversal.

| Ability / Feature | What it does | Best used for |

The counterweight to all that power is a dedicated antagonist. The new pursuer is described as relentless and highly adaptive, engineered specifically to match the Warden's strength. Encounters with it escalate into large-scale, cinematic fights rather than skirmishes — which means raw aggression alone won't carry you.
